# Intent

## What is being built

A CodePen-style in-browser HTML, CSS, and JavaScript playground. Editors and a sandboxed preview stay in the browser. Share via URL hash. No auth, database, or compile server.

## For whom

Anyone who wants to sketch and share a frontend snippet without signing in or standing up a backend.

## Values

Correctness. Client-side only.

## Taste

Short files. Obvious seams. No extra servers.

## Process weight

Low
